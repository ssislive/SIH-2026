COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How do I update my profile? +

Open your Profile page and select Edit Profile. You can update your personal and farming information there.

How can I check market prices? +

Open Market Prices from the navigation menu to explore crop prices and filter information according to your needs.

How do I sell my produce? +

Use Sell Produce to create a listing containing your crop, quantity and other relevant information.

How can I contact a buyer? +

Buyers and selling opportunities can be explored through the relevant marketplace sections of KrishiSetu.

What if I find incorrect information? +

Send us a support request using the form on this page and describe the issue clearly.
